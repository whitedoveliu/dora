# Restaurant booking

A reservation is a commitment with a cancellation window. Learn the terms before you hold the table.

## Before booking

- Party size, including children and high chairs.
- Date, and an arrival window rather than a single minute; plus or minus 30 minutes is the default.
- Occasion, seating preference, and accessibility needs.
- Dietary constraints the restaurant must know in advance.
- The name the reservation should be under, and a phone number that will answer.

## Availability

1. Check the restaurant's own booking page first; a marketplace often holds only part of the inventory.
2. Read the terms before confirming: deposit, card hold, cancellation window, no-show fee, and the maximum table time.
3. For parties above the online maximum, use the restaurant's phone or form. Do not split the party silently.
4. Verify the hours for that specific date, including holidays and closure days.

## Decide versus ask

| Situation | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Inside the member's window, no deposit, party size standard | Book it and report |
| Deposit or card hold required | Ask first, showing the amount and the cancellation terms |
| Hard-to-get venue, or a date more than 30 days out | Offer two or three options and let the member choose |
| Party above the online maximum, or a private room | Ask before calling; the terms are usually different |
| The member's preferred time is unavailable | Offer the nearest alternatives, do not silently move the time |

## Confirmation

Collect and send in one message: restaurant name and address, date and time, party size, name on the booking, confirmation number, deposit if any, cancellation deadline, and any table-time limit.

## Changes and cancellations

- Cancelling or moving a booked table is a commitment change. Tell the member, then act on their instruction inside the fee window.
- Set a wake before the cancellation deadline whenever a fee applies.
- Never leave a table held the member cannot use, and never hold two tables for the same slot.
- If the restaurant cancels, report it with the alternative you already secured or the options you found.
