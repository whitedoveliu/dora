# Flight booking

Flights are the least forgiving purchase you make. Compare on total cost and terms, then confirm the final state.

## Inputs

- Origin and destination airports, dates, and how much date flexibility exists.
- Passengers, cabin, and baggage needs.
- Loyalty programs, seat preferences, and any corporate travel rule.
- For international travel: passport validity, visa needs, and the exact spelling of the name on the travel document.

## Searching

- Use real-time flight search for the market view, then the airline's own site for the fare that can actually be ticketed.
- Compare total cost, not base fare.
- Watch for separate tickets sold as one itinerary; they are not protected if a leg is missed.

## Rules

- **Connections:** prefer one stop or fewer. Minimum connection times: 60 minutes domestic, 90 minutes domestic to international, 120 minutes international to international or across terminals.
- **Self-transfers** on separate tickets need a buffer of three hours or more, and the member must be told the risk.
- **Overnight and red-eye:** state the arrival day and the cost of the lost night.
- **Baggage:** personal item, carry-on, and checked, per leg, on the actual fare class. Basic economy fares often cannot be changed at all.
- **Name spelling must match the travel document** exactly. Fix it before purchase, never after.

## Total cost

| Line | What to include |
| --- | --- |
| Fare | Per passenger, per direction |
| Taxes and fees | Carrier surcharges and airport fees |
| Seat | Only when the member needs a specific seat |
| Bags | Carry-on and checked, both directions |
| Flexibility | Change and cancellation terms, in money |
| Ground | Transfer to and from the airport when it is material |

## Final-state rule

Before any purchase, show one block: flight numbers, dates, times, and airports; passengers with names as ticketed; cabin and fare class; total charged today; baggage included; change and cancellation terms; and the payment label.

- The member must see that block and approve it. "Book me the cheapest" is not approval for a specific itinerary.
- Never complete a non-refundable purchase the member has not seen.
- Award bookings show points plus taxes, alongside the cash alternative.
- If the price changed since the search, re-confirm; do not absorb the difference silently.

## After booking

- Keep the itinerary in `/downloads`. Report the confirmation numbers and the airline's own record locator.
- Schedule wakes for online check-in at 24 hours and for any change or cancellation deadline.
- If a schedule change arrives later, report the new times and the rebooking options rather than accepting a bad connection automatically.
